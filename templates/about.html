About, Help & Contact

About RISE - Results Intelligence & Student Evaluation

RISE is a predictive analytics platform designed to forecast student academic performance in South African schools. Our mission is to leverage machine learning to identify at-risk learners, enabling targeted interventions and improved educational outcomes. We support the goals of the National Development Plan (NDP) 2030 and address systemic challenges such as dropout rates, inequality, and resource allocation.

The platform integrates historical and contemporary educational data, stakeholder insights, and ethical frameworks to ensure a commercially viable and socially impactful solution. We are committed to data privacy, fairness, transparency, and accountability in all our operations.
